Who we are

A small studio, under a green moon.

Founded with a love for weird ideas and playable stories, GreenMoonDev builds mobile games that feel personal. We work fast, ship often, and chase the kind of moments that stick with players long after they close the app.

From sneaking past a Brainrot mother to escaping a hunting monster — our worlds are small, strange, and made by hand.
