Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

GreenMoonDev ("we", "our", or "us") operates mobile games published on Google Play, including Steal a Baby Brainrot and Horror Escape Brainrot Monster (the "Games"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Our Games may collect the following information automatically:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language, region)
  • Advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID)
  • App usage data, gameplay events, crash reports
  • Approximate location (country-level, derived from IP)

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email or phone number through gameplay.

How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the Games
  • To display advertisements through third-party ad networks
  • To analyze gameplay and fix bugs
  • To comply with legal obligations

Third-Party Services

Our Games use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, including:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements
  • Google Play Services — for core functionality
  • Firebase — for analytics and crash reporting

These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of such information.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your Choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's settings under Google → Ads.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
